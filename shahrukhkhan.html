wikipedia says: Shah Rukh Khan[a] (pronounced [ˈʃaːɦɾʊx xäːn] ⓘ; born as Shahrukh Khan on 2 November 1965), popularly known by the initials SRK, is an Indian actor and film producer renowned for his work in Hindi cinema. Referred to in the media as the "Baadshah of Bollywood" and "King Khan",[b] he has appeared in more than 90 films and earned numerous accolades, including a National Film Award and 15 Filmfare Awards.
